What it does

Everything you need to stay on track

Track every dose

  • Check off each dose with one tap
  • Any number of doses a day, on the days you choose
  • Instructions like "take with food," plus notes on why you take each one

Reminders that follow up

  • Reminders at the times you set
  • Optional follow-up if a dose is late, or "remind me in 30 minutes"
  • Mark a dose as taken right from the reminder
  • Quiet hours with silent notifications

Stay safe

  • Warnings for medications that shouldn't be taken together
  • Pairings for medications that go together
  • Supply tracking with low-supply alerts
  • One-tap calls to your pharmacy

See your progress

  • A monthly calendar of complete, partial and missed days
  • Completion percentages and your most-missed doses
  • PDF reports for 7, 30 or 90 days, or dates you choose, to share with your doctor or caregiver
Privacy

Your health information stays on your iPhone

Your medications, schedules and history never leave your device unless you choose to share a report. You don't need an account to use the app.

Read the full privacy policy

  • No account, no ads and no third-party analytics.
  • We never receive your medication names, doses or history.
  • Included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup, so a new phone can restore your medications.
  • Reports are created on your phone and sent only to the people you choose.
