Leanto Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

  • What you put in Leanto stays on your iPhone.
  • You don't need an account.
  • We (ForgeApp Studio LLC, "we" or "us") never receive your routines, your check-offs or anything else you enter.
  • Leanto has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • We never sell or share your information.

The rest of this page explains each part in more detail. This policy covers the Leanto app for iPhone ("the app"), including test versions shared through TestFlight.

1. What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter or choose in the app is saved only on your iPhone. This includes:

  • Your routines: their names, steps, days, times and reminder levels.
  • When you checked off, skipped or moved a routine, and how much reminding each routine gets.
  • Your settings, such as quiet hours, snooze length and reminder sounds.
  • Whether you finished setup, and the age range Apple shares (see section 4).

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

2. What we don't collect

We don't collect, receive or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or other contact details.
  • Your routines, steps, check-offs or any health information.
  • Your location or your contacts.
  • Your advertising identifier. The app never asks to track you.
  • Information about how you use the app's screens and features.

3. Reminders and alarms

Reminders and alarms are created and delivered by your iPhone itself. Buttons on a reminder or alarm, such as "Mark done" or "Snooze", are handled on your iPhone too.

Reminders can show a routine's name on your Lock Screen. If you'd rather they didn't, turn on "Hide details on Lock Screen" in the app's Settings, or for one routine on its own screen. Routines that mention medication have this turned on from the start.

You can turn reminders and alarms off at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

4. Your age range

During setup, the app can ask Apple whether you are under 18 or 18 and over. Apple asks you first, and you can say no. Apple shares only the range, never your birthday. The app uses the answer to turn on extra protections for people under 18. The answer stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us.

5. iCloud Backup and new iPhones

If iCloud Backup is on, the app's information is included in your iPhone's backup, so you can keep it when you set up a new iPhone. The same is true for backups to a computer and for moving directly from one iPhone to another. Apple handles these backups under its own privacy policy. We can't see or open them.

Leanto doesn't sync with iCloud yet. If we add an option to keep a copy of your routines in your own iCloud, it will be off until you turn it on, and we will update this policy first. Health-related information, such as medication check-ins, will stay on your iPhone.

6. Test versions (TestFlight)

If you test Leanto through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple may share some information with us so we can fix problems. This can include crash reports, basic details about your device and the app version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. TestFlight may also share the email address you were invited with. Apple's TestFlight privacy information explains this in full. We use this information only to improve Leanto.

7. Children and teens

Leanto is meant for people aged 13 and older. Because the app doesn't collect personal information, we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us.

8. Keeping your information safe

Because your information is on your iPhone, your iPhone's own security protects it, including your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. We can't see, recover or restore your information, including if your iPhone is lost, stolen or damaged.

9. Deleting your information

You can delete any routine in the app. To delete everything, delete the app from your iPhone. Copies in your iPhone backups are managed by you through Apple.

10. Other services

The app has no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and shows no ads. It relies only on Apple, for the App Store, TestFlight, reminders, alarms, age range and backups, under Apple's privacy policy.

11. The Leanto waitlist on this website

This part is about our website, not the app. If you join the Leanto waitlist on forgeappstudio.com, we receive your email address, who you're signing up as, and whether you'd like to help test. It is stored in a private Google Sheet that only we can open. We use it only to tell you about Leanto and to invite you to test, and we never share or sell it. To leave the list or have your details deleted, email us.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to know what information we hold about you and to ask us to delete it. The app doesn't send us your information, so we hold nothing from it. For the waitlist, we hold only what is described in section 11. To make a request, contact us.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, we will also say so in the app before it takes effect.

14. Contact us

If you have a question or request about this policy, please contact us:
