How we build

Three promises in every app

Private by default

Your information stays on your device. We don't run ad networks or third-party analytics, and we never sell data.

Calm, clear design

Plain language, large tap targets and no clutter. Reminders help you without nagging.

Honest pricing

Prices are shown up front, trials don't surprise you, and you can cancel any time in your Apple Account.
